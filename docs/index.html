HOW I APPROACH THE WORK

Make the reasoning
easy to follow.

A polished answer is only the beginning. I look at the evidence, the user's constraints, and what the response leaves uncertain.

  1. 01

    Start with the actual request.

    Separate the task, the constraints, and the information available.

  2. 02

    Show the evidence.

    Connect every rating or preference to a specific observation.

  3. 03

    Know the limits.

    Distinguish a supported conclusion from an assumption—and a demonstration from a measured result.
